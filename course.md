Subject: AI Ethics, Governance and Deployment 

Subject Overview 
The "AI Ethics, Governance, and Deployment" module includes component of the Diploma in Artificial Intelligence program, designed to address the ethical, regulatory, and practical aspects of AI implementation. This course covers essential modules that ensure students are well-versed in responsible AI practices and effective deployment strategies.

Total Credits: 3 

Topics Covered: 
Module 1: Ethical Considerations in AI 
Module 2: AI Bias & Fairness 
Module 3: AI Governance and Regulations 
Module 4: Model Deployment with Flask/Django & Cloud Services 
Module 5: AI Project Lifecycle & DevOps for AI
