COURSE PLAN: AI ETHICS, GOVERNANCE AND DEPLOYMENT
Total Credits: 3

SUBJECT OVERVIEW
The "AI Ethics, Governance, and Deployment" module includes component of the Diploma in Artificial Intelligence program, designed to address the ethical, regulatory, and practical aspects of AI implementation. This course covers essential modules that ensure students are well-versed in responsible AI practices and effective deployment strategies.

DETAILED SYLLABUS

Module 1: Ethical Considerations in AI
1.1 Core Ethical Principles: Transparency, Accountability, Fairness, and Non-maleficence in AI systems.
1.2 Societal Impact Analysis: Evaluating the effects of AI on employment, privacy, and social dynamics.
1.3 Ethical Frameworks: Reviewing guidelines from global organizations (IEEE, EU AI Act, OECD).
1.4 Responsible AI Design: Incorporating "Ethics by Design" methodologies in the development phase.

Module 2: AI Bias & Fairness
2.1 Understanding Bias: Identifying Data bias, Historical bias, and Algorithmic bias.
2.2 Fairness Metrics: Measuring Disparate Impact, Equal Opportunity, and Statistical Parity.
2.3 Bias Mitigation Strategies: Techniques for Pre-processing (re-weighting), In-processing, and Post-processing.
2.4 Explainability (XAI): Implementing LIME and SHAP to interpret "Black Box" models.

Module 3: AI Governance and Regulations
3.1 Regulatory Landscape: Deep dive into GDPR, CCPA, and emerging AI-specific laws.
3.2 Data Privacy & Security: Differential Privacy, Federated Learning concepts, and Data Anonymization techniques.
3.3 Corporate Governance: Establishing AI Review Boards and Risk Management frameworks for AI products.
3.4 Audit & Compliance: Conducting AI system audits and maintaining documentation for regulatory compliance.

Module 4: Model Deployment with Flask/Django & Cloud Services
4.1 RESTful API Development: Wrapping ML models using Flask/FastAPI or Django for backend integration.
4.2 Containerization: Using Docker to create consistent runtime environments for reproducibility.
4.3 Cloud Deployment Strategies: Deploying containerized models on platforms like AWS (EC2/Lambda), Azure, or Google Cloud.
4.4 Scalability & Security: Handling concurrent requests and securing API endpoints (API Keys, Rate Limiting).

Module 5: AI Project Lifecycle & DevOps for AI (MLOps)
5.1 MLOps Fundamentals: Differences between DevOps and MLOps; The ML lifecycle loop.
5.2 Version Control for Data & Models: Using tools like DVC (Data Version Control) and Git.
5.3 Continuous Integration/Deployment (CI/CD): Automating testing and deployment pipelines for ML models.
5.4 Model Monitoring: Tracking model drift (Data drift, Concept drift) and performance degradation in production.
